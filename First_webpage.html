Why Music Is So Great To Me.

How did music grow on me?

Music has always had a great impact on me. My older brother started introducing it into my life as I grew up, specifically showing me the more interesting side of music rather than just what was trending. I had a phone, but I never really had social media to understand what most people were growing up with. The more my brother showed me, the more I fell in love. Soon I was begging my parents to buy me a pair of headphones. The first time I heard music be the only thing playing into my ears was sort of heavenly. Every instrument, every beat, every tiny sound in the song, was all thought about while it was made.

What genres did I fall into?

Once I finally had my beautiful headphones, and music to play, I couldn't stop discovering more and more about what I liked. It sort of taught me about myself and the people around me. The genres I liked usually had a way of making me feel comforted and helped me find what people I felt comfortable with. The songs I listened to 5 years ago, are mostly the same to what I still listen to now. I've spent so much time inside the music I love so much, I've completely stopped having and actual genre I fall into. In my opinion, if it sounds good, it's a good song. By far my music tastes now range from Korn to Illit, two completely different bands, but they have good music.
